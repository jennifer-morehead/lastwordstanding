🧠 How to Play 1. Start the game and receive your first word. 2. Enter a valid English word that:
• Starts with the last letter of the previous word
• Follows the game’s word rules and the daily rule 3. Submit before time runs out. 4. Repeat until you can’t make a valid move.

One invalid word, or running out of time, and the game is over.

🏆 Scoring & Leaderboards
• Your score increases with every valid word you chain.
• When your run ends, your final score is submitted to the leaderboard.
• Compare your best runs against other players and see who’s truly the Last Word Standing.

Leaderboards reset on a daily cycle, giving everyone a fresh chance to compete.

⚡ Why You’ll Like It
• Easy to learn, hard to master
• Short, high-intensity rounds
• Strategic word choices matter
• Perfect for quick play sessions
• Competition without complicated rules

💡Inspired by the classic Japanese word game Shiritori
